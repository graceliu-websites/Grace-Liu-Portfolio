03

Problem Statement

By gauging market-fit, we ensure there is market need for the product involved with our startup as we get closer to building a product that people love .

Main Problems

According to research findings, online shoppers often face:

  • Information overload when evaluating endless products.
  • Decision fatigue when faced with too many purchase options.
  • Postponing or abandoning purchases on an e-commerce platform altogether.

Main Objectives

Through our workflow and app, we aim to help online shoppers achieve:

  • Streamlined Browsing with reduced need to compare products across countless tabs.
  • Confident Check-outs with surfacing consensus reviews and expert advice on each product.
Core Takeaway

Online shoppers need a reliable, efficient, and personalized way to find products that genuinely fit their needs without the cognitive overwhelm that hinders confident purchases.

05

Competitive Analysis

Evaluating how well each competitor e-commerce app caters to the needs and preferences, goals and pain points, as well as areas for improvement based on our user personas.

EVALUATION CRITERIA

Onboarding Experience

How quickly can users start browsing?
What tools are in place to help guide new users on the platform?

Navigation Structure

How intuitive is the navigation of the system?
Are important functionalities easily found?

Product Discovery

How efficient are the search tools, categorical filters, visual hierarchy of product listings, and content layouts of product specs?

Checkout Experience

How smooth is the checkout process, guest checkout, shopping cart management? Is there cost transparency and various types of payment options?

Personalization

How easy can users do new account setup, account personalization, view availability of order history and active order status, and use wish list features?

Unique Value

What special features, tools, or services does the platform offer?

User Testing

Conducting user testing through structured interviews to evaluate the usability and effectiveness of our high-fidelity prototypes and identify areas for improvement and validation of design decisions.

Objective

Gather user feedback and insights through structured interviews to evaluate the usability and effectiveness of Techtronics high-fidelity prototypes.

Scope

Assess design strategy for early prototyping phase addressing key challenges: confusing site architecture, overwhelming product catalogs, and cluttered product pages.

Methodology

Conduct 3 user interviews with targeted participants to observe interactions, validate design assumptions, and identify areas for iterative improvement.

Outcome

Generate actionable insights to inform design decisions and enhance user experience across mobile and desktop platforms.

Participant Overview

We targeted participants matching our core personas —budget-conscious, tech-savvy— and ensured diversity across technology literacy, product knowledge, and shopping habits.

Participant #1

Demographics:

  • Age: 40
  • Gender: Female
  • Occupation: Retired

Alignment with User Personas:

  • Spends a lot of time comparing brands, functions, and pricing of different products
  • Not tech savvy, needs assistance when shopping for tech products
  • Appreciates e-commerce loyalty membership features like cashback
Participant #2

Demographics:

  • Age: 26
  • Gender: Male
  • Occupation: Product Designer

Alignment with User Personas:

  • Very knowledgeable on tech products
  • Frequently does research on products before purchasing from e-commerce platforms
  • Appreciates shopping recommendations and personalization features
Participant #3

Demographics:

  • Age: 21
  • Gender: Male
  • Occupation: Student

Alignment with User Personas:

  • Shops occasionally on e-commerce platforms
  • Is not very confident when it comes to shopping for electronic products
  • Typically just compares customer reviews and the overall impression of a product

Actionable Recommendations

Pop-up Message

A one-time pop-up introducing Gizmo as Techtronics’ AI Shopping Guide to add visual hierarchy and overcome user confusion on Homepage

Recommendation I

Enriched Product Cards

Increase card contrast and add rich media, key specs, and accessory clarity to Gizmo Picks to enhance a more personalized, trustworthy, and informed user comparison.

Recommendation II

Image-based Options

Replace text-only options with image-based cards in Gizmo Questions to add visual clarity for users when defining their preferences to Gizmo.

Recommendation III

Catalogue Filters

Replace Gizmo Picks in Product Catalog with a persistent filter with an active indicator to satisfy the need for more apparent filter options.
