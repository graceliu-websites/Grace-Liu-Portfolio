01

Overview

A guided shopping assistant for people who find buying electronics overwhelming.

Problem

75% of interviewed shoppers postpone or abandon purchases because of information overload and decision fatigue.

Goal

Streamlined browsing and confident checkout, without comparing products across countless tabs.

Solution

Gizmo, a guided shopping assistant that asks a few questions, recommends 3–5 products, and walks users through checkout.

Core Takeaway

Shoppers need a reliable, efficient, personalized way to find products that fit their needs without the overwhelm.

Competitor audit

What competitors do

I audited Amazon and Best Buy on onboarding, navigation, discovery, checkout, personalization and unique value. Amazon wins on variety and checkout but feels cluttered. Best Buy is more focused on tech but narrower and slower.

Takeaway: neither one helps a hesitant shopper decide.

Sprint 5 · Iteration

What we changed

Four recommendations came out of testing. Each one went straight into the next version of the design.

Pop-up Message

A one-time pop-up introducing Gizmo as Techtronics' AI Shopping Guide to add visual hierarchy and overcome user confusion on Homepage.

Change I

Enriched Product Cards

Increase card contrast and add rich media, key specs, and accessory clarity to Gizmo Picks for a more personalized, trustworthy, and informed comparison.

Change II

Image-based Options

Replace text-only options with image-based cards in Gizmo Questions to add visual clarity when users define their preferences.

Change III

Catalogue Filters

Replace Gizmo Picks in Product Catalog with a persistent filter with an active indicator to satisfy the need for more apparent filter options.
