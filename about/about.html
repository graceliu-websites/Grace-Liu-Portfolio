About Me

Hi! I'm Grace,
A UX designer based in Maryland

Hi, I'm Grace! I've loved drawing, animation, and visual storytelling since I was young. From illustrating for UCLA's Daily Bruin to building my own 3D simulator, I've always enjoyed bringing creative ideas to life. That passion eventually led me to UX/UI design, where I get to combine art with practical problem-solving.

I have a bachelor's degree in History from UCLA and studied UX/UI design at UCLA Extension. I'm currently pursuing my master's in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Maryland, where I've been exploring user research, accessibility, and interaction design. I'm also working with Public Finance Firm (PFF), LLC on Treasora, designing solutions for real-world financial workflows.
