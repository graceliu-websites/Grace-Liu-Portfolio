About Me

Hi! I'm Grace,
A UX designer based in Maryland

I am a UX designer and illustrator pursuing my M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Maryland, College Park. My background in history has taught me to see problems from multiple perspectives, while illustrating for Daily Bruin at the University of California, Los Angeles and building front-end projects drew me toward User Experience. Having family members with disabilities and limited access to technology, alongside my own frequent frustrations with complicated digital workflows, makes accessibility and inclusivity personal to me. My goal is to simplify complex experiences through empathetic design, making technology more easier and enjoyable for everyone to use.
