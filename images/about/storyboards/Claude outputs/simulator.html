How I Built It

Six steps from a menu on a website to a game you can play. I used ChatGPT to plan and debug along the way.

  1. 1

    Find the data

    I started with the Ben & Jerry's flavor menu on their official website: flavor names, descriptions, ingredients, allergens, calories and ratings.

    • Americone Dream
    • Cherry Garcia
    • Chunky Monkey
    • Half Baked
    • Phish Food
  2. 2

    Sketch the concept

    I drew the flow by hand first. You arrive at a welcome screen with a freezer, zoom in to a display of flavor cones, and pick a cone to see its nutrition details next to a comparison chart. A refresh button loads more flavors.

    I first built that idea as an indoor shop. It didn't make it into the final game, so the menu moved outside to an ice cream truck in a park. [Add one sentence on why you changed it.]

  3. 3

    Build the 3D world

    I used Three.js to set up the park, the truck, the characters and the sky in the browser.

    Drag any model to turn it around.

  4. 4

    Add sound

    I used Tone.js to play a lofi ice cream truck song in the background. The audio came from a YouTube video. Browsers block music that starts on its own, so players click once to turn it on.

  5. 5

    Make it playable

    Three.js orbit controls and some JavaScript let the player walk around the park with the W, A, S and D keys and look around. On-screen prompts guide them: Enter to continue, Esc to leave the simulator, and a click to turn on the music.

  6. 6

    Build the menu

    I built the menu with HTML and CSS, then loaded the flavor data from a JSON file so each flavor fills in its own details.
