About Me

Hi! I'm Grace,
A UX designer based in Maryland

I've always loved art, animation, and motion graphics, from illustrating for UCLA's Daily Bruin to building an interactive 3D Ben & Jerry's simulator. I enjoy bringing creative ideas to life.

My interest in UX/UI began with small frustrations: a friend struggling to share their location, my mom navigating her banking app, me hunting for customer support online. If unclear instructions can frustrate people without disabilities like us, they're far harder for those with disabilities. That drew me to UX/UI, where I combine creativity with problem-solving to make digital experiences intuitive and simple.

My journey began at UCLA Extension, where I learned foundational UX/UI skills. Now, as a master's student in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Maryland, I am going deeper into user research, accessibility, and interaction design.

I've turned that learning into my own case studies: banking and e-commerce apps designed from scratch, plus experiments with how AI tools can support my creative process.

One of my most exciting experiences is working with Public Finance Firm (PFF), LLC on its Treasora platform, where I'm learning to design for real clients and complex financial workflows. With my history background and growing interest in accessibility, I've come to see good design as how well it works for the people using it.
