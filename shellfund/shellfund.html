Preliminary Problem Statement

College students face unique financial challenges: irregular income, tight budgets, student loans, shared living expenses, limited credit history, and low financial literacy. A well-designed personal-finance app for this audience must empower students to:

Image generated by ChatGPT-5.1

Track Cash Flow

Monitor fluctuating income, daily expenses, refunds, and financial aid disbursements in one unified view.

Build Safe Habits

Establish healthy money habits through adaptive budgeting, personal saving goals, and credit-building tracks.

Manage Shared Expenses

Easily co-manage shared living spaces, split bills with roommates, and handle group expenses transparently.

Student Loans & Tuition

Gain absolute clarity over complex tuition timelines, outstanding student loans, and debt payoff planning.

Contextual Learning

Master complex financial concepts using bite-sized micro-learning steps and proactive contextual nudges.

THE DESIGN GOAL

To build an experience that is helpful, low-friction, non-judgmental, privacy-respecting, and mobile-first — beautifully tuned to students' complex schedules and mental models.

Positive Design Guidelines

Using common ethical concerns in technology and in UX design, and based on ethical considerations of generated personas, ChatGPT is asked to play as devil's advocate to identify dark patterns that will be countered by a positive design framework supporting ShellFund's ethics.

Data Privacy & Security

Dark Pattern

Coerced or hidden data practices paired with difficult platform exit paths and poor user control.

Positive Guideline

Autonomy and transparency paired with purposeful, values-aligned, and inclusive privacy design.

Financial Transparency & Advice Integrity

Dark Pattern

Disguised or biased financial recommendations, misdirection, and high ambiguity in financial advice.

Positive Guideline

Purposeful guidance paired with deep financial understanding and transparent advisory pathways.

Psychological & Behavioral Design

Dark Pattern

Emotional manipulation, forced gamification, and harmful social comparison mechanics.

Positive Guideline

Emotional support, positive affect, and joyful, non-pressuring exploration of money habits.

Accessibility & Inclusivity

Dark Pattern

Exclusionary interfaces, rigid normative defaults, and strong majority demographic bias.

Positive Guideline

Inclusive, highly accessible design flexible enough to accommodate human-diverse financial experiences.

ShellFund Requirements Blueprint

System Architecture & Structural Directives

01 | Root Concept

A mobile-first personal finance app designed for college students who are new to money management; blends security, transparency, motivation, and accessibility into a single student-centered experience.

02 | Business Goals

  • Empower students to develop lifelong financial literacy and independence.
  • Reduce student overspending and missed payment incidents.
  • Encourage consistent savings behavior through ease-of-use.
  • Establish partnerships with universities, banks, and loan service providers as trusted financial integration points.
  • Position ShellFund as the go-to financial wellness app for the student market.

03 | Target Users

ShellFund serves U.S.-based college students across diverse age ranges, including financially stressed, low-literacy users needing basic control, high-income independent students seeking advanced tools, and those managing complex finances such as aid, loans, and disbursements.

04 | Target System Configuration

  • Platform: Mobile App (iOS & Android - prototype iPhone 14)
  • Design System: Interactive HTML Specimens
  • Integrations: Financial Aid Systems, Loan Servicer APIs, bank APIs, Payment Processors, University Billing Systems
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, screen-reader friendly, high-contrast themes

05 | Usability Objectives

  • Task success rate: 90% of first-time users can set up a budget within 10 minutes.
  • Efficiency: 80% of users can link at least one bank or loan account within 5 minutes.
  • Engagement: At least 60% of users return to check their budget weekly after 1 month.
  • Satisfaction: Average rating of 4.0/5 or higher on the System Usability Scale (SUS).

06 | Functional Specs

  • Dashboard financial overview
  • ShellAI financial mentor
  • Spending insights and trends
  • Budget setup, tracking, and category manager
  • Savings goals tracking with progress visuals
  • Quick money actions
  • Notification system for personalized smart alerts and reminders
  • Adjust settings and personalize the app

07 | User Goals

  • Get an overview of their finances from the Dashboard
  • Review and understand their spending patterns
  • Manage their budget and spending categories
  • Track and update their savings goals
  • Use ShellAl for financial guidance or questions
  • Adjust settings and personalize the app
  • Transact money or take quick financial actions

08 | Constraints

  • Limited student attention span: UI must be simple, intuitive, and playful.
  • Privacy: Students control account access at a granular level.
  • Technical dependency: Relies on third-party APIs (banks, loan servicers, universities) that may limit real-time functionality.
  • Time-to-value: Onboarding must deliver visible utility within first session.
  • Scope control: Prioritize essential MVP features (e.g., budgeting, expense tracking, financial health view).

Accessibility Review (WCAG 2.2)

A table of Issues, WCAG Principles Violated, Users Excluded, and Recommended Fixes are prompted through ChatGPT-5 based on the initial high-fidelity mockup of ShellFund.

Key Risk Area WCAG Principles Users Potentially Excluded Targeted Remediation
Visual Contrast & Hierarchy
Low contrast on progress bars/pill tags, text truncation at 200% zoom, and DOM order speech mismatches. 		Perceivable 1.4.3 Robust 4.1.2 Low-vision, color-blind, & screen-reader users Users in bright ambient light or utilizing screen magnifiers.
  • Enforce ≥ 4.5:1 contrast ratios across progress fills & badges.
  • Support text scaling up to 200% without container clipping.
  • Align the DOM speech order with visual card reading hierarchy.
Sole Reliance on Color
Payment statuses (green check, grey pending) and active navigation tabs distinguished strictly by color. 		Perceivable 1.4.1 Color-deficient & monochrome users Users unable to perceive red/green financial indicators.
  • Pair every status color with explicit icons & text (✔ Completed, ⚠ Late).
  • Add underlines or filled icon variants to active navigation tabs.
Tap Targets & Focus Indicators
Small icon buttons (bell, profile) below 44×44px and missing focus outlines during keyboard/switch navigation. 		Operable 2.5.5 Operable 2.4.7 Motor-impaired & keyboard users Users with tremors, older adults, and switch-device navigators.
  • Expand all interactive touch footprints to 44×44px minimum.
  • Add high-contrast 2px focus indicator rings on all interactive elements.
  • Convert flat history rows into distinct keyboard-accessible list items.
Financial Jargon & Action Stress
Complex formulas (APR, interest payoff) lack micro-help, generic button labels, and harsh error-red CTA buttons. 		Understandable 3.1.3 Understandable 3.3.2 ESL, neurodivergent, & new users First-generation college students and anxiety-prone learners.
  • Provide contextual tooltips explaining financial jargon and payoff math.
  • Use explicit action labels (“View Loan Details” vs. “Details”).
  • Replace alarming red CTA buttons with calm, supportive brand hues.

A Summary of Key Risks the initial prototype posts for Users with Accessibility issues.

Contrast & Hierarchy Friction

Users with low vision, color-blindness, motor impairments, and screen-reader users may struggle.

Color Overreliance

Payment statuses and navigation tabs rely solely on color alone(WCAG failure); secondary icon/text cues are needed.

Undersized & Missing Tap Targets

Tap areas and focus indicators (e.g, touch footprints (<44px) and focus indicators) exclude motor & switch users and need improvement.

Cognitive & Language Unexplained

Some financial terms (e.g, APR, payoff) lack clarity for ESL , neurodivergent, low-literacy users, or even new users.
